Why DeepHUD

A focus tool that shows up, then gets out of the way.

Always in sight, never in the way

Resize it, snap it to a corner, dial the opacity down, or enable click-through mode to work right underneath it.

Focus your way

A stopwatch, a countdown, Deep Work blocks, Pomodoro cycles, or a fully custom work-and-break rhythm.

Turns focus into insight

Daily and weekly totals, streaks, session averages, and a clear picture of where your time actually goes.

Stays on your device

No account, no cloud, no telemetry. Sessions, projects and settings live in a local database — nothing leaves your machine.

Organized by project and task

Attach every session to a project and task, reuse previous entries, and see exactly where your focused time is going.

How it works

1

Name your focus

Attach a project and a task before you start, or reuse ones from an earlier session.

2

Start the session

Pick a mode and let the HUD float above whatever you're working in — a shortcut starts your default session instantly.

3

Watch it add up

History, streaks and daily goals build automatically in the background, with nothing to log by hand.

Every rhythm, covered

Pick a built-in preset or shape your own — DeepHUD adapts to how you actually work.

Pomodoro

Work in focused intervals followed by restorative breaks — the classic 25/5 rhythm, or a custom schedule of your own.

Deep Work

Uninterrupted focus blocks of 25, 50, 90, or 120 minutes, with a project and task attached to keep every session purposeful.

Custom Intervals

Set your own work and break durations, skip an interval when you need to, and let the next one start automatically.

Built for real workdays

The small things that make a timer worth trusting day after day.

Smart idle detection

DeepHUD notices when you step away and pauses or excludes that time automatically, so your statistics reflect the time you actually spent focused.

Focus notifications

Five-minute warnings, session-complete alerts, break reminders and daily-goal notifications — with sound on or off, your choice.

Global keyboard shortcuts

Start, pause, reset, hide the HUD, or launch a Deep Work session without leaving whatever app you're in — try Ctrl + Alt + Space.

Full session history

Search, filter, edit or delete any past session, then export the whole history — or just what you need — as CSV or JSON.

Backup and restore

Create a complete backup of your sessions, projects, goals and settings, and restore it whenever you set up on a new machine.

See where your focus goes

A quiet dashboard tracks daily and weekly totals, session averages, your longest sessions, and how time splits across projects — without asking you to fill in a single field.

Set a daily focus target and watch your progress fill in live — DeepHUD keeps count of your streak automatically, so consistency builds on its own.

Private by design

DeepHUD works completely offline. There's no account to create and no server to talk to — your sessions, projects and settings live in a local database on your device, and stay there.

  • No account required
  • Works fully offline
  • No telemetry or usage analytics
  • No cloud sync, no network client
  • Local SQLite database, always yours
